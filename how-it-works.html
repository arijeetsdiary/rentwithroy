How it works

From application to your first delivery.

Four steps, no paperwork headaches. Most drivers are on the road within three days.

01

Apply online

Fill in the short form at Apply. Name, contact details, licence type, delivery platform. Takes under 2 minutes, no paperwork.

02

Quick approval

Roy reviews your licence and delivery platform account. No credit check. You'll hear back within 24 hours.

03

Sign and collect

Sign the rental agreement digitally, then collect the car at Tarneit VIC 3029. Exact address given after approval.

04

Start earning

Deliver with UberEats, Deliveroo, DoorDash, or use the car personally. Pay one simple weekly rate.

Eligibility

What you need to apply

Valid licence

Australian driver's licence, or an overseas licence with an official English translation.

Platform account

An active UberEats, Deliveroo or DoorDash account, or a valid reason for personal use.

Aged 20+

You need to be at least 20 years old to apply for a rental.

Work rights

Legally permitted to work in Australia. Any visa type that allows work is fine.
